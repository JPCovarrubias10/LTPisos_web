About Us

A specialized firm in industrial slab design, with over 20 years of experience and presence in Chile, Peru, Colombia, Costa Rica, Mexico and the United States

Technical expertise and independence

LTPisos is a company specialized in industrial slab and concrete pavement design, with over 20 years of experience, over 100 projects executed and over 2.5 million m² built in Chile, Peru, Colombia, Costa Rica, Mexico and the United States.

We work alongside clients, developers, construction companies and engineering firms, providing specialized technical expertise in ground-supported structures for demanding operations in the logistics, retail and industrial sectors.

A firm with recognized technical backing, proven international presence and over two decades of operating projects.

Engineering in service of the project

LTPisos' differentiation lies in its technical depth and independence from products and suppliers. This allows analyzing each project from an engineering perspective, comparing slab alternatives based on loads, type of operation, geometry, flatness level, soil conditions, budget and constructability.

The result is solutions tailored to project reality, not generic proposals or conditioned by a specific technology.

There is no single solution that works for all projects. The choice of slab type is a technical decision that must be evaluated case by case with engineering criteria.

Industrial Slab Technologies

Being a product-independent consultant, the recommendation is defined case by case according to project engineering: operation, loads, flatness, budget and soil conditions

1

Post-tensioned Slab

High-performance system for heavy traffic. Reduces or eliminates saw-cut joints and is especially convenient in demanding operations with small hard-wheeled equipment, where slab continuity and durability are critical.

No joints Heavy traffic Super flat
2

Shrinkage-Compensating or Volumetric Stability

Large panels without saw-cut joints, suitable for heavy traffic operations and slabs with high flatness requirements. Requires careful design and execution of joints, edges and materials to ensure performance.

No saw-cut joints Heavy traffic Alta planeidad
3

Strategically Reinforced Slabs

Widely used system in distribution centers. It considers controlled-size panels with load transfer bars at joints and a layout designed to improve performance against traffic and slab durability.

2.5 - 4.5 m High durability Versatile
4

Smaller Size, Interlocking Slabs (2 to 3 m)

Proven solution for warehouses with tall racks and medium or low traffic. Reducing panel size improves curling control and load transfer. Adding structural fiber further improves performance. Not recommended for heavy traffic due to high joint maintenance, but is an economical option for low-traffic slabs.

2 - 3 m Curling control Tall racks
5

Fiber Reinforced Concrete

Concrete reinforced with structural fiber significantly increases the load-bearing capacity of the slab without visible signs of cracking, while also providing greater design flexibility. FRC is compatible with various paving solutions and, in the presence of visible cracks or fissures, the fiber helps contain them, optimizing performance under applied loads.

Greater capacity
6

Vehicle Traffic Pavements

Design of pavements for areas with truck traffic, forklifts and yard vehicles, considering fatigue, load transfer and structural support according to the operational conditions of each facility. The OptiPave System technology is usually employed, which optimizes performance and reduces costs.

Heavy traffic Fatigue design Exterior pavements
